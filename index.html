Andrew Ross Sorkin, author of Too Big to Fail

November

New York Times columnist and author, Andrew Ross Sorkin has been described as the most famous financial journalist of his generation. His book, Too Big to Fail, was the first book to capture the collapse of major financial institutions during the 2008 crisis.

The Economist, The Financial Times, and BusinessWeek all named Too Big to Fail one of the best books of the year. The book was published by Viking on October 20, 2009.
