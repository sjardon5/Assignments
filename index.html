Andrew Ross Sorkin

Author of Too Big to Fail

New York Times columnist and author Andrew Ross Sorkin has been described as "the most famous financial journalist of his generation." His book, Too Big to Fail, was widely praised and adapted into a television movie.

The Economist, the Financial Times, and Businessweek named Too Big to Fail one of the best books of the year. The book was published by Viking on October 20, 2009.

Movie cast

  1. William Hurt as Hank Paulson
  2. Paul Giamatti as Ben Bernanke
  3. Billy Crudup as Timothy Geithner
