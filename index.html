Andrew Ross Sorkin author of Too Big to Fail

November

New York Times columnist and author, Andrew Ross Sorkin has been described as the most famous financial journalist of his generation His book, Too Big to Fail, was the first in a series of books about the financial crisis.

The Economist, The Financial Times, and the Business week all named Too Big to Fail one of the best books of the year. The book was published by Viking October 20, 2009. The book was a bestseller and was well received by critics.
